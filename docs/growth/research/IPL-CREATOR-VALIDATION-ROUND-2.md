## How evidence is graded

- **Indexed Ulike content:** Modash's [Ulike sponsored-post examples](https://www.modash.io/content-library/brands/ulike-examples) provide a named account, dated quoted caption and *single-post* metrics. These substantiate the existence of a third-party attributed sponsored claim, **not** an independently inspected video or proof of results.
- **Creator identity:** a second source (news, representation site, profile directory) confirms identity and/or handle.
- **UK audience:** use *only* dated estimated **follower geography**, not creator residence or Ulike UK sponsorship. Third-party vendor estimates are provisional and not first-party Instagram Insights.
- **Engagement:** preserve each platform/source/date/method separately. A post's 200 likes and 30,000 views are **not** an account engagement rate.
- **Review confirmation:** `ipl_review_confirmed` **requires inspecting an attributable, original first-person video/article**. No new person reaches that bar in this pass.

2. **Competition:** All five have visible **Ulike-sponsored work**; possible exclusivity or conflicts need checking before a CYPH/1 partnership.

5. **Claims:** A creator's personal hair-reduction, pain-free or efficacy statement must **not** be reused as a CYPH/1 clinical/product claim.
