### Candidate selection policy

**Before adding any new row to the development database:**

1. Confirm the correct identity, original public creator or editorial profile and stable public `sourceUrl` (not merely a handle inferred from an index).
2. Verify the original content, publication date, actual device and firsthand-use wording; keep sponsored posts distinct from independent testing.
3. Check professional/sponsorship restrictions, UK relevance and primary social channel; follower location remains `unknown` without real evidence.
4. Use the five-dimension existing scoring algorithm. Do not award UK audience or engagement scores from market location, published post counters or directory follower estimates.
5. Add approved research in **development only**, after a separately reviewed batch, validation, audited manual import and explicit confirmation.
6. Preserve `research_only`, no outreach, no sending, no lead-contact database enrichment.
