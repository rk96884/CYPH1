## Purpose and approval boundary

Find UK retail, marketplace and complementary-channel businesses that may be interested in stocking or recommending a compliant, home-use IPL hair-removal device. This is *not* an outreach authorisation. Store public, attributable information, distinguish a published supplier application from willingness to accept CYPH/1, and obtain approval before any contact, samples, marketing or database import.

The existing creator-oriented Growth Intelligence score must **not** be silently reused for commercial businesses. Proposed Agent C evidence fields: retailer/customer fit; public supplier application; explicit vs inferred electrical/beauty-device acceptance; UK customer evidence; commercial model; terms and margin; fulfilment/returns capabilities; source date; confidence; unknowns; human-review status.

## Readiness and economics

**Product gate:** Before any retailer onboarding or commercial claims, confirm UK conformity evidence applicable to the final K-803 configuration, approved user instructions, labels, warranty/returns, insured responsibilities, available stock, product imagery, fulfilment reliability and final pricing. Supplier interest is not a substitute for these checks.

**Margin gate:** Model on the intended £74.99 retail price using the conservative £24.78 historical landed baseline, reconfirmed VAT position, postage and fulfilment, payment fees, refunds/warranty reserve, marketing/affiliate commission and seller fees. Do not invent wholesale margin or forecast sales. A small direct-fulfilment/referral pilot could be operationally more practical than wholesale, but still requires economics and explicit acceptance.

**Illustrative commission only:** Dripscan's published 12% applied to £74.99 is £9.00 per order (rounded), *before* considering tax treatment, any other fees, fulfilment, returns or settlement rules. It is not confirmed net cost or profitability.

## Exclusions and caution

- Retailer affiliate programmes that recruit *people to promote the retailer* are not evidence that the retailer accepts **CYPH/1 as a supplier**.
- Do not conflate retailer geographical location with UK customer/audience share.
- Retailers' published claims about reach, membership and seller metrics are first-party assertions, not independently audited evidence.
- Professional laser providers and wax suppliers may perceive at-home IPL as competitive. Do not assume they will refer buyers.
- Do not load candidates into development Growth storage until an Agent C-specific schema/approval workflow and deduplication are reviewed.
