This is the strongest relationship. Vietnam's Ministry of Health [advertising disclosure for IBP Holdings/Emmié](https://imda.moh.gov.vn/cong-khai-nd-ht-quang-cao-ttbyt?_congkhaiquangcaottbyt_WAR_lienthongdvcportlet_congKhaiQuangCaoId=204906&_congkhaiquangcaottbyt_WAR_lienthongdvcportlet_jspPage=%2Fhtml%2Fportlet%2Fvanbancongkhaiqc%2Fadd_congbo.jsp&_congkhaiquangcaottbyt_WAR_lienthongdvcportlet_view=true&_congkhaiquangcaottbyt_WAR_lienthongdvcportlet_viewCongKhaiDaCongBo=true&p_p_col_count=1&p_p_col_id=column-1&p_p_id=congkhaiquangcaottbyt&p_p_lifecycle=0&p_p_mode=view) lists **model SL-B329**, retail **EM448**, and manufacturer **Shenzhen Semlamp Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd**. The medical equipment [registration entry](https://imda.moh.gov.vn/web/guest/van-ban-cong-bo?_vanbancongbo_WAR_trangthietbiyteportlet_doanhNghiepId=261011&_vanbancongbo_WAR_trangthietbiyteportlet_hoSoId=611805&_vanbancongbo_WAR_trangthietbiyteportlet_jspPage=%2Fhtml%2Fttbyte%2Fportlet%2Fvanbancongbo%2Fxemhoso_tuybien.jsp&_vanbancongbo_WAR_trangthietbiyteportlet_vanBanId=528675&p_p_col_count=1&p_p_col_id=column-1&p_p_id=vanbancongbo_WAR_trangthietbiyteportlet&p_p_lifecycle=0&p_p_mode=view) repeats that pairing. The [retailer product page](https://www.happyskin.vn/may-triet-long-ipl-therapy-device-for-hair-removal-em448-pro-ice-infinite-glide) explicitly names Semlamp as producer and cites registration number **250003620/PCBB-HCM**.

**Risk flag:** Emmié distinguishes EM448, EM448+ and possible revisions; colour, advertised settings and energy may differ from a factory quoted configuration. Treat the **documented model lineage** as confirmed, but not every feature or batch as interchangeable.

Potential review leads: the [Emmié brand's customer reviews](https://www.happyskin.vn/testimonial) include accounts reporting several treatments, but those are **unverified retailer testimonials**, not named independent reviewers, controlled efficacy testing or UK creator leads.

### 2. Strong: Semlamp SL-B352-2 → Japanese KXP/Yete

Japan's [Rakuten KXP/Yete sales listing](https://item.rakuten.co.jp/chance-k/ylpkk20260530/) explicitly prints **KXP / SL-B352-2**. The [Japanese pricing/product aggregator](https://shoply.co.jp/%E3%82%B9%E3%83%A0%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BA%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AD%E3%83%B3%20%E5%AE%B6%E5%BA%AD%E7%94%A8%20%E8%84%B1%E6%AF%9B%20%E8%84%B1%E6%AF%9B%E5%99%A8%20bare%20%E3%83%96%E3%83%A9%E3%83%83%E3%82%AF%E3%81%AE%E4%BE%A1%E6%A0%BC%E3%81%A8%E6%9C%80%E5%AE%89%E5%80%A4) explicitly identifies **Shenzhen Semlamp** as maker of this **KXP SL-B352-2**, and an [international mirror of the Japanese item](https://www.u-buy.co.uk/productjp/QQ67G6MIU-yet-hair-removal-device-ipl-light-hair-removal-device-sapphire-cooling-continuous-irradiation-equipped-with-skin-sensor-6-levels) exposes the same model.

This is *not* a general claim that all Yete devices are Semlamp. The brand name appears across dissimilar IPL models from other manufacturers. Confirm an original device/box photo listing the manufacturer before treating the full supply-chain link as proven.

The specific SL-B352-2 is part of Semlamp's [FDA 510(k) K260518 listed model family](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfPMN/pmn.cfm?ID=K260518), decision **21 April 2026**. This is **US regulatory status for Semlamp's named devices**, not evidence of UKCA/UK consumer market conformity or that a KXP retail batch has identical labelling.

No qualifying named firsthand reviewer of the specific KXP version was verified. Existing sources are marketplace listings and customer-review aggregates only.

### 3. Lead, not confirmed: IONKA K-902 → SkinRevolve

The [SkinRevolve UK-origin product on Ubuy](https://www.ubuy.mg/en/productuk/TSA2LFQ1W-ipl-hair-removal-device-21j) lists model **K-902**, 21J, HR/DPL/MG heads, Chinese country of origin. **IONKA's own catalogue** separately [lists a K-902 with DPL and other multi-function modes](https://www.tzwm.com/). A separate [UK eBay SkinRevolve listing](https://www.ebay.co.uk/itm/168788283766) supports that a SkinRevolve 21J product is offered by a reseller, but does not say it was made by IONKA.

**Do not mark this brand–manufacturer relationship confirmed**. Retailer-provided model numbers can be repackaged, incorrectly attributed or shared across unrelated vendors. Need a manufacturer label/manual, certificate identifying the actual importer, GTIN, supplier invoice or direct IONKA written confirmation.

No named long-term independent review of SkinRevolve K-902 was located in this pass; a [retail aggregate](https://www.ubuy.cy/en/productuk/TSA2LFQ1W-ipl-hair-removal-device-21j) explicitly says no editorial reviews found, which is *not proof of global absence*.

### 4. No established alias: IONKA K-803

[IONKA K-803 manufacturer listing](https://tzwm.com/ProDetail.aspx?Proid=29) provides **238g, 143 × 87 × 48 mm, 48W, five power levels, dual flash and 999,999 flashes**. The [manufacturer's OEM Alibaba listing](https://www.alibaba.com/product-detail/Factory-Wholesale-IPL-Hair-Removal-Device_1601516157138.html) shows **K-803** for private-label supply, without identifying any retail client.

No independently verifiable retail alias or named creator trial found. Match future leads using device ID, specific dimensions, adapter power, control layout and manual, but **never infer same origin just from appearance**.

## Implications for CYPH/1

1. We now have **two credible Semlamp retail identities**, useful for assessing how formerly quoted supplier devices are presented, packaged and reviewed under consumer-facing brands. They are **not the chosen CYPH/1 IONKA K-803**.
2. **SkinRevolve is the next investigative lead** for IONKA K-902; the risk of falsely treating a model-name match as a confirmed OEM relationship remains high.
3. **K-803 is still a retail visibility blind spot**. Ask IONKA for a non-confidential list of retail model aliases and label-family names before searching influencers under invented brand names.
4. Distributor-supplied promotional claims and consumer testimonials cannot be repurposed as independent clinical or safety proof.
5. Initial UK creator identification is **not improved by overseas retail matches alone**. We should prioritise independent reviewers of comparably priced products without claiming exact same hardware.
