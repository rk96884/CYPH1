## Findings for CYPH/1

1. **IONKA K-803/K-902 have a public-review visibility gap** under those exact model identifiers. A private-label retail brand might use a different name; establishing device equivalence requires matching documentary evidence, not appearance alone.
2. **Laura Gladman is a stronger specialist-review lead** than several generic influencer-directory candidates because she documents actual treatment routines, real-use drawbacks, and comparative device tests.
3. **Do not confuse "Chinese manufactured" with "unbranded OEM"**. JOVS, Ulike, BoSidin, AMINZER and INNZA have distinct brands, devices, disclosures and quality evidence. They are comparison candidates, not proven identical hardware.
4. **Editorial and influencer pipelines are separate.** Reviewers may be more appropriate for future evidence-led editorial submissions than creator campaigns, pending disclosure and independent editorial safeguards.
5. **No compliance shortcut:** FDA listing for Semlamp's SL series or IONKA's FZ series does not establish UK conformity or safety for CYPH/1's chosen K-803. Keep the existing supplier-specific UK compliance review separate from influencer research.
