# CYPH/1 Claims and Educational Source Register

**Status:** Approved educational foundations for the pre-launch website  
**Reviewed:** 21 August 2026  
**Scope:** Hair-cycle and general IPL educational statements only

## Publication rules

- General educational statements must be supported by an authoritative or peer-reviewed source.
- General IPL evidence must not be presented as evidence for the performance of a future CYPH/1 product.
- Product specifications, results, safety, regulatory status and clinical claims remain unpublished until verified for the selected product and explicitly approved.
- Supplier marketing material is not independent evidence.
- The site must not imply that IPL identifies the growth phase of an individual hair.

## Source register

### SRC-001 — NCBI Bookshelf: Anatomy, Hair Follicle

- Publisher: National Center for Biotechnology Information
- URL: <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK470321/>
- Supports: four-stage terminology and concise descriptions of anagen, catagen, telogen and exogen.
- Notes: Exogen is described as sometimes being considered a telogen sub-phase; this is why the website uses “commonly described”.

### SRC-002 — Integrative and Mechanistic Approach to the Hair Growth Cycle and Hair Loss

- Publication: *International Journal of Molecular Sciences* (2023)
- URL: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9917549/>
- Supports: anagen as growth, catagen as regression, telogen as rest and exogen as shedding.
- Notes: Peer-reviewed review article used to corroborate the four-stage educational model.

### SRC-003 — Photoepilation and Skin Photorejuvenation: An Update

- Publication: *Journal of Cutaneous and Aesthetic Surgery* (2017)
- URL: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5475414/>
- Supports: IPL as a light-based photoepilation technology and the greater susceptibility of follicles during anagen.
- Notes: General scientific background only; it does not substantiate the performance of a future CYPH/1 device.

### SRC-004 — The Role of Lasers and Intense Pulsed Light Technology in Dermatology

- Publication: *Clinical, Cosmetic and Investigational Dermatology* (2016)
- URL: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4745852/>
- Supports: the photoepilation mechanism and the use of multiple treatment sessions to improve clinical outcome.
- Notes: General IPL evidence only. No numerical outcome from this source is approved for CYPH/1 marketing.

## Explicitly unapproved claims

The following remain unapproved unless evidence for the final CYPH/1 product is recorded and separately authorised:

- permanent hair removal or permanent results
- pain-free or painless treatment
- clinical-performance percentages or treatment timelines
- exact treatment intervals or number of sessions
- suitability for particular skin tones or hair colours
- exact fluence, joules, cooling temperature, flash count or treatment-window size
- FDA, UKCA or other regulatory status
- comparisons or superiority claims
