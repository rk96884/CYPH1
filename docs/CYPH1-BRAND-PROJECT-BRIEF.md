# CYPH/1 — Brand & Project Brief

**Status:** Pre-launch / product validation  
**Market:** United Kingdom initially  
**Primary domain:** cyph1.co.uk  
**Last updated:** August 2026

---

## 1. Project Overview

CYPH/1 is a new direct-to-consumer beauty-technology brand being developed
initially around premium at-home hair removal.

The first product is expected to be a portable, home-use IPL hair-removal
device.

The project is currently in product and manufacturer validation. Multiple
OEM/ODM manufacturers are being assessed before a final device, specification,
fulfilment model and commercial proposition are selected.

The website must therefore be built around the CYPH/1 brand rather than around
any particular manufacturer's current device.

---

**1**

identity.

## 5. Brand Story

Hair grows through a repeating cycle:

1. Anagen — active growth
2. Catagen — transition
3. Telogen — rest
4. Exogen — shedding

IPL treatment relies on repeated treatments across the hair-growth cycle,
allowing treatment to coincide with hairs entering active growth.

CYPH/1 takes its name from this principle:

**CY**cle.  
**PH**ase.  
**1**.

**Target the Growth.**

### Scientific-language rule

Never imply that the device can detect whether an individual hair is currently
in the anagen phase unless a future product has independently verified
technology capable of doing so.

"Target the Growth" is a brand proposition, not a claim that CYPH/1
automatically identifies individual hair-growth phases.

## 7. Visual Identity

### Current approved direction

The preferred CYPH/1 logo direction is:

- futuristic / contemporary typography
- minimalist construction
- angled contemporary hair-follicle symbol replacing the conventional slash
- follicle symbol follows approximately the angle/direction of a forward slash
- restrained rather than illustrative
- suitable for very small reproduction on a physical device
- suitable for packaging, web, social avatars and favicon adaptation

The visual identity should feel engineered rather than decorative.

### Current colour direction

Preferred visual territory:

- near-black
- deep purple
- dark aubergine
- metallic lavender
- soft white
- subtle silver / metallic highlights

The existing concept uses a dark near-black/deep-purple background with
white/metallic-lavender typography.

Exact colour tokens are NOT yet final and should initially be maintained as
design tokens so they can be adjusted centrally.

### Avoid

Do not introduce generic beauty imagery into the identity, including:

- women's-leg silhouettes
- sparkles
- literal laser beams
- generic hair strands
- pink beauty gradients without purpose
- medical crosses
- unnecessarily complex follicle illustrations

## 12. Fulfilment Strategy

Four fulfilment models have been considered:

1. Generic manufacturer dropshipping
2. Private-label manufacturer dropshipping
3. Manufacturer-held branded inventory with direct fulfilment
4. Bulk import to UK + UK 3PL fulfilment

Current preference during validation is to minimise inventory exposure.

Longer term, UK-held inventory fulfilled through a suitable 3PL may provide
better control over:

- delivery speed
- customer experience
- returns
- warranty handling
- packaging
- stock management

No fulfilment model is final.

## 17. Accessibility

Accessibility is a first-class requirement.

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where practical.

Requirements include:

- semantic HTML
- keyboard accessibility
- visible focus states
- sufficient colour contrast
- meaningful alt text
- reduced-motion consideration
- correctly associated form labels
- accessible validation/errors
- sensible heading hierarchy
- no essential information communicated through colour alone

---

## 18. Performance

The site should feel premium partly because it is fast.

Prioritise:

- Core Web Vitals
- minimal JavaScript
- responsive images
- modern image formats
- sensible lazy loading
- limited font payload
- no unnecessary third-party scripts
- no animation that materially harms performance

## 21. Claims & Trust

CYPH/1 should build trust through evidence rather than exaggerated advertising.

Avoid unsupported phrases such as:

- "#1 IPL"
- "best IPL"
- "100% painless"
- "permanent results"
- "clinically proven"

unless robust evidence exists for the exact claim and product.

Supplier marketing copy is NOT evidence.

Any future regulatory, performance or clinical claim should be documented
before publication.
