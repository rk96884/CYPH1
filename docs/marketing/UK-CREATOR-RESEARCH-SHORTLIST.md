## Purpose

Maintain a pre-launch research pool of UK creators, UGC specialists, beauty-tech reviewers and authority/editorial contacts who may fit CYPH/1 after the final product, verified instructions, suitability parameters and substantiated claims are known.

This is not an approved outreach list. Re-verify profiles, audience data, conflicts, prices and availability immediately before any contact.

## Commercial interpretation

A listed UGC price generally pays for **content production**, not automatically for publication to the creator's audience. Organic posting, paid-ad usage, whitelisting, exclusivity, raw footage, additional hooks, revisions and extended usage rights may all be separately priced.

Where no reliable public rate was found, the table says **Not publicly verified** rather than estimating a creator-specific fee.

Current market context only: public UK guidance places entry UGC around £50–£150/video, standard work around £150–£500, and premium work from £500 upward. This is a planning benchmark, not a rate attributed to any individual below.

## Budget planning benchmark

Do not use creator-specific estimates where no rate is public. For scenario planning only, current UK UGC guidance suggests approximately:

- entry creator: £50–£150 per simple video;
- standard working creator: £150–£500 per ad-ready video;
- premium creator: £500–£2,500 per video;
- audience distribution, usage rights, whitelisting, raw footage and exclusivity can add material cost.

A separate live-market report of public brand UGC listings reported a US$200 median opening offer and US$50–$550 interquartile range on 14 September 2026. Public brand offers tend to skew lower than privately negotiated work.

## Final-product gate

Do **not** contact or commission this shortlist until:

1. the final CYPH/1 product is selected;
2. verified instructions and final suitability parameters are available;
3. substantiated product claims are entered in the claims register;
4. the intended price/market positioning is sufficiently clear to assess creator fit; and
5. the collaboration brief, disclosure rules and content-usage rights are approved.

After those gates are met, rescore each candidate for:

- audience fit;
- content quality;
- beauty-tech/category credibility;
- commercial accessibility;
- CYPH/1 brand fit;
- competitor conflicts;
- final-product suitability/audience relevance; and
- willingness to support a longitudinal rather than one-day product trial.

## Intended launch mix

The initial working hypothesis is:

- 3–5 micro/UGC creators;
- 1 larger credible beauty creator;
- 2–3 editorial/PR or professional relationships.

Prioritise a **multi-week CYPH/1 cycle diary** over one-day unboxings where the final product instructions and claims permit it. Any creator content must use clear advertising disclosure where required and must not expand CYPH/1 claims beyond the approved register.

## Re-verification

Follower counts, pricing, engagement, availability and brand conflicts change quickly. Recheck all commercial and audience information immediately before outreach. Record the date and source of any quoted rate used in negotiation.
