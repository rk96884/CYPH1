# CYPH/1 marketing content review checklist

**Status:** Required before publication  
**Owner:** CYPH/1  
**Applies to:** Website articles, organic social posts, video, carousels, email, creator briefs and paid media

## How to use this checklist

Create one completed copy for each asset or tightly related asset set. Record any exception in the notes and obtain explicit approval before publication.

An unchecked required item means the content is **not ready to publish**.

## 1. Purpose and audience

- [ ] The asset has one clear communication objective.
- [ ] The intended audience is defined without inferring sensitive medical or personal information.
- [ ] The principal next action is clear and proportionate.
- [ ] The content provides genuine educational or brand value rather than existing only to attract clicks.
- [ ] The format and length are appropriate for the selected channel.

## 2. Claims and evidence

- [ ] Every factual claim is listed below with its claim ID and source.
- [ ] Each claim is approved in `docs/content/CLAIMS-SOURCE-REGISTER.md` or has received separate written approval.
- [ ] Any paraphrase remains faithful to the approved statement and does not expand its meaning.
- [ ] General IPL evidence is not presented as evidence for a future CYPH/1 product.
- [ ] The content does not state or imply that IPL identifies the growth phase of an individual hair.
- [ ] No unapproved specification, result, treatment interval, suitability statement, safety claim or regulatory status appears.
- [ ] The content avoids unsupported “permanent”, “pain-free”, “clinically proven”, “best”, “expert” or superiority language.
- [ ] Numerical claims retain their relevant population, conditions, timeframe and source.
- [ ] Qualifications and disclaimers are sufficiently prominent and understandable.
- [ ] The publication date, review date and sources are visible where the format requires them.

## 3. Pre-launch and product status

- [ ] The asset does not imply that the final product, manufacturer, specification, price or launch date has been selected unless verified.
- [ ] Concept-product imagery carries the approved `Product direction — final design subject to change` disclaimer.
- [ ] The concept image is not used as proof of performance, features or physical design.
- [ ] Conditional champagne-metal styling is limited to approved hardware relationships, campaign details or the hair illustration.
- [ ] The asset does not create an offer, allocation, discount or scarcity promise that CYPH/1 has not approved and can honour.

## 4. Brand and visual system

- [ ] The approved logo asset and clear space are used correctly.
- [ ] Michroma is used for display typography and Manrope for body copy where the format supports custom fonts.
- [ ] Colour, typography and spacing follow the approved design tokens.
- [ ] Signal Lavender remains the primary accent.
- [ ] Champagne or gold is not used for body text, routine controls or broad backgrounds.
- [ ] The composition has one clear focal point and sufficient negative space.
- [ ] Cycle geometry and phase notation carry meaning rather than acting as decorative noise.
- [ ] Scientific diagrams use flat colours and direct labels.
- [ ] Metallic effects, glow and animation remain restrained.
- [ ] No generic laser beams, sparkles, clinical imagery or stock hair-removal poses have been introduced.

## 5. Copy and editorial quality

- [ ] British English is used consistently.
- [ ] The opening communicates the value quickly without clickbait.
- [ ] Headings and captions are concise and grammatically complete.
- [ ] Technical language is explained in plain language.
- [ ] The tone is considered, precise and credible rather than sensational.
- [ ] The CTA accurately describes what happens next.
- [ ] The early-access invitation does not imply payment, pre-order or guaranteed product access.
- [ ] Spelling, punctuation, dates, URLs and brand naming have been proofread.
- [ ] `CYPH/1`, `Cycle. Phase. One.` and campaign terminology are styled consistently.

## 6. Accessibility and inclusive communication

- [ ] Essential information is not communicated by colour alone.
- [ ] Informative images have concise, meaningful alternative text.
- [ ] Decorative images are marked decorative or omitted from the text alternative.
- [ ] Video includes accurate captions, checked manually after any automated transcription.
- [ ] Essential visual information in video is also communicated in audio, captions or the accompanying description.
- [ ] On-screen text remains visible long enough to read.
- [ ] Text contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA expectations.
- [ ] Text is not embedded at a size that becomes unreadable on a typical mobile screen.
- [ ] Flashing, rapid cuts and unnecessary looping motion are avoided.
- [ ] The content remains understandable when motion is disabled.
- [ ] Language and imagery avoid stereotypes, shame and exclusionary beauty messaging.

## 7. Channel and advertising compliance

- [ ] Paid, gifted, affiliate or otherwise controlled creator content is clearly and prominently identifiable as advertising.
- [ ] Advertising disclosure appears upfront rather than behind “more”, a profile bio or ambiguous hashtags.
- [ ] Any creator has received the approved claims and prohibited-claims brief.
- [ ] Creator usage rights, editing rights, term and placement have been agreed before reuse.
- [ ] Platform-specific restrictions have been checked immediately before publication.
- [ ] The content does not target or exploit sensitive medical, body-image or personal characteristics.
- [ ] A paid-media budget and stop condition are approved before launch.

## 8. Consent, privacy and data

- [ ] Marketing consent is collected through the approved double-opt-in flow.
- [ ] The CTA links to the approved early-access form and Privacy Notice.
- [ ] No personal data appears in URLs, UTM parameters, screenshots or creative assets.
- [ ] Subscriber email addresses and CRM records are not visible in production materials.
- [ ] No non-essential analytics tag, advertising pixel or behavioural retargeting has been added without a consent and privacy review.
- [ ] Any data export is limited to the approved purpose and stored appropriately.
- [ ] Unsubscribe and withdrawal routes remain clear where applicable.

## 9. Links and attribution

- [ ] The destination page is live, correct and relevant to the asset.
- [ ] The destination works over HTTPS and uses the canonical `www.cyph1.co.uk` host.
- [ ] UTM parameters follow `docs/marketing/ACQUISITION-MEASUREMENT-PLAN.md`.
- [ ] `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` and `utm_content` use lowercase approved values.
- [ ] The UTM link has been tested without changing the page canonical URL.
- [ ] Profile, bio, caption and button destinations are mutually consistent.
- [ ] Link-shortening or redirection services have not obscured an unnecessary or untrusted destination.

## 10. Technical and visual QA

- [ ] The final asset dimensions and file type match the platform placement.
- [ ] Text and key graphics remain inside the documented safe area.
- [ ] Raster output is sharp at its delivery resolution.
- [ ] SVG or vector source remains available where appropriate.
- [ ] File size is proportionate and compression does not damage the logo, typography or line art.
- [ ] The asset has been previewed on a representative mobile screen.
- [ ] The asset has been checked in light and dark platform interfaces where relevant.
- [ ] Video audio level, captions, poster frame and final frame have been checked.
- [ ] The published-preview view contains no accidental crop, overflow or interface obstruction.
- [ ] The destination page and early-access journey have been tested immediately before scheduling.

## 11. Measurement plan

- [ ] The primary success measure is defined.
- [ ] A secondary engagement measure is defined.
- [ ] The campaign link has been entered in the link register.
- [ ] Internal testing activity will be excluded from acquisition reporting.
- [ ] Paid content will be judged by confirmed subscribers rather than unconfirmed form submissions.
- [ ] The review date and continue/revise/pause/scale decision point are scheduled.
