# CYPH/1 four-week pre-launch content calendar

**Status:** Proposed for approval  
**Campaign period:** 24 August–20 September 2026  
**Campaign territory:** Know the cycle  
**Primary objective:** Qualified website visits and confirmed early-access subscribers  
**Primary audience:** UK adults researching at-home IPL and considered beauty technology

## Content guardrails

- Use only claims approved in [CLAIMS-SOURCE-REGISTER.md](../content/CLAIMS-SOURCE-REGISTER.md).
- General IPL evidence must not be presented as evidence for a future CYPH/1 device.
- Do not state or imply that IPL detects the growth phase of an individual hair.
- Do not publish product specifications, performance figures, treatment intervals, suitability advice or safety claims.
- Do not use “permanent”, “pain-free”, “clinically proven”, “best” or “expert” without separate substantiation and approval.
- Concept imagery must retain the approved final-design disclaimer.
- Use subtitles and meaningful text alternatives. Avoid essential information appearing only in audio or graphics.
- Each post should have one principal action, normally to learn more or join early access.

## Publishing rhythm

The opening month deliberately prioritises quality over daily posting:

- six short-form videos;
- four educational carousels;
- two long-form website articles; and
- one optional end-of-month subscriber update after the articles are live.

Instagram and TikTok can use the same core video with platform-appropriate edits. Carousels can also be adapted into Pinterest pins after their information and design have been approved.

## Short-form production briefs

### SF-01 — Hair does not grow all at once

**Hook:** “Hair does not grow all at once.”  
**Purpose:** Introduce the cycle as the central CYPH/1 educational idea.  
**Length:** 15–20 seconds.  
**Visual direction:** Begin with the approved circular Anagen graphic, then reveal the four approved follicle illustrations in sequence.  
**On-screen sequence:**

1. Hair does not grow all at once.
2. The cycle is commonly described in four stages.
3. Anagen. Catagen. Telogen. Exogen.
4. Know the cycle.

**Caption draft:**

> Hair grows through a repeating cycle commonly described as anagen, catagen, telogen and exogen. CYPH/1 begins by making that cycle clearer. Learn more and join early access at cyph1.co.uk.

**Claims:** EDU-001.  
**Source note:** SRC-001 and SRC-002.  
**Destination:** Homepage until AR-01 is live; update the profile link to AR-01 afterwards.  
**UTM content:** `hair-cycle-video-01`.

### SF-02 — Why CYPH/1 begins with anagen

**Hook:** “Phase one: active growth.”  
**Purpose:** Connect the brand name to approved anagen education without claiming phase detection.  
**Length:** 20–25 seconds.  
**Visual direction:** Use the Anagen card and gold follicle illustration, with the number `01` and “ACTIVE GROWTH”.  
**On-screen sequence:**

1. Anagen is the active-growth phase.
2. The follicle is producing the hair shaft.
3. Light-based hair reduction is associated with active growth.
4. Treatment is repeated across the cycle.

**Caption draft:**

> Anagen is the active-growth phase. It is also why light-based hair reduction is approached as a repeated routine across the natural cycle—not as a one-off event. General IPL education only; no final CYPH/1 product claims are being made.

**Claims:** EDU-002 and EDU-006.  
**Source note:** SRC-001–004.  
**Destination:** Homepage early-access section.  
**UTM content:** `anagen-video-01`.

### SF-03 — IPL does not read a hair’s phase

**Hook:** “A useful distinction: IPL does not identify an individual hair’s growth phase.”  
**Purpose:** Prevent a likely misunderstanding of “Target the growth”.  
**Length:** 20–25 seconds.  
**Visual direction:** A clean target motif that resolves into four asynchronously positioned follicle illustrations; do not animate a device scanning or detecting follicles.  
**On-screen sequence:**

1. IPL does not identify an individual hair’s phase.
2. Different hairs can be at different points in the cycle.
3. That is why treatment is repeated over time.
4. A considered routine, not a shortcut.

**Caption draft:**

> “Target the growth” describes the treatment principle and the importance of timing across the cycle. It does not mean that an IPL device can detect the phase of an individual hair.

**Claims:** EDU-007, with EDU-006 as context.  
**Source note:** SRC-003 and SRC-004.  
**Destination:** AR-01.  
**UTM content:** `phase-detection-clarification-video-01`.

### SF-04 — Catagen in one clear idea

**Hook:** “What happens after active growth?”  
**Purpose:** Explain catagen simply and establish a repeatable stage-by-stage series.  
**Length:** 12–18 seconds.  
**Visual direction:** Use only the approved Catagen follicle illustration and its transition label.  
**On-screen sequence:**

1. 02 / Catagen
2. Active growth stops.
3. The follicle enters a short transition or regression phase.

**Caption draft:**

> Catagen is the short transition or regression phase in which active hair growth stops. It is the second stage in our four-part guide to the cycle.

**Claims:** EDU-003.  
**Source note:** SRC-001 and SRC-002.  
**Destination:** AR-01.  
**UTM content:** `catagen-video-01`.

### SF-05 — Telogen is rest

**Hook:** “Rest is part of the cycle.”  
**Purpose:** Explain telogen without implying that the hair has already shed.  
**Length:** 12–18 seconds.  
**Visual direction:** Approved Telogen illustration with restrained circular motion around the outer cycle, not the follicle.  
**On-screen sequence:**

1. 03 / Telogen
2. The follicle rests.
3. The existing hair is no longer actively growing.

**Caption draft:**

> Telogen is the resting phase. The existing hair remains, but it is no longer actively growing. Next in the cycle: release and shedding.

**Claims:** EDU-004.  
**Source note:** SRC-001 and SRC-002.  
**Destination:** AR-01.  
**UTM content:** `telogen-video-01`.

### SF-06 — Exogen: release and shedding

**Hook:** “The stage that completes the visual story.”  
**Purpose:** Complete the four-stage series while preserving the qualification around terminology.  
**Length:** 15–20 seconds.  
**Visual direction:** Use the approved Exogen illustration with the existing separated-hair detail. Avoid dramatic shedding imagery.  
**On-screen sequence:**

1. 04 / Exogen
2. The existing hair is released and shed.
3. Some sources treat exogen as part of telogen.
4. The cycle continues.

**Caption draft:**

> Exogen describes the release and shedding of the existing hair. Some sources treat it as part of telogen, which is why we describe the four-stage model as one common way of explaining the cycle.

**Claims:** EDU-005.  
**Source note:** SRC-001 and SRC-002.  
**Destination:** AR-01 or homepage early-access section.  
**UTM content:** `exogen-video-01`.

## Review and production workflow

Each asset must complete these checks before scheduling:

1. Copy matches the approved claim wording or remains a faithful, non-expansive paraphrase.
2. Claim IDs and source links are recorded in the working file.
3. No claim has migrated from general IPL evidence to future-product performance.
4. The visual uses approved CYPH/1 assets and tokens.
5. Essential information is available as text, captions and appropriate alternative text.
6. The destination URL works and the UTM values follow the agreed taxonomy.
7. Concept-product disclaimers remain present wherever concept imagery is used.
8. A final mobile preview has been checked before publication.

## End-of-month review

Review the four-week batch by content ID and record:

- reach and qualified website visits;
- saves, shares and meaningful comments;
- video completion rate;
- early-access form submissions;
- confirmed subscribers;
- double-opt-in completion rate;
- unsubscribes or complaints; and
- the clearest audience or message learning.

Do not select a winner from reach alone. Prefer the content that produces useful engagement, relevant visits and confirmed consent.
